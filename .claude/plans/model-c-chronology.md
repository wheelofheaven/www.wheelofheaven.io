Founder-ratified reconciliation of the corpus's Adamite-creation / Eden /
antediluvian chronology. Supersedes all earlier per-entry conventions.
Surfaced while working the flagged-issues ledger (serpent/adamites), then
decided point-by-point with the founder.

## The two-stage Eden model (the ratified consequence)

The 930-year lifespans forbid Genesis Adam being both created at 11,375 BCE
and expelled at 8,850 BCE. So:

- **First Adamites (the human SPECIES / finalized *Homo sapiens sapiens*):**
  c. **11,375 BCE**, the **Virgo–Leo boundary / opening of Leo**. This is
  the 666-generations-of-20-years anchor (Rev 13:18; `number-of-man`,
  `timeline/age-of-leo`). NEVER "late Leo," NEVER "mid-Leo."
- **Collective Eden phase** (Israel-team teaching of the Adamite
  population): spans the **Age of Leo**, 11,010–8,850 BCE.
- **Genesis Adam & Eve** (the named founding individuals): the **close of
  the Eden phase**, c. **8,850 BCE** (late Leo / Leo–Cancer boundary) —
  DISTINCT from and ~2,500 yr after the first Adamites. Founder note:
  canonically there were earlier human species-cycles before the Adam
  cycle (each cycle supersedes the prior — why the Elohim feared humans).
- **Eden disclosure / Fall / expulsion (Genesis 3):** Leo–Cancer boundary,
  c. 8,850 BCE.
- **Antediluvian patriarchs (Adam 930 → Noah), Nephilim, pre-Flood
  civilisation:** the **Age of Cancer**, 8,850–6,690 BCE. Its ~2,160-yr
  span accommodates Genesis's own 1,656-yr antediluvian chronology
  (the corpus treats the cumulative genealogy sum as telescoped; see
  `antediluvian`).
- **Tree-of-Life patriarchal grant (Phase II, post-Eden):** 8,850–6,690 BCE.
- **Flood:** 6,690 BCE (Cancer/Gemini boundary).
- **Antediluvian WORLD as a whole (first Adamites → Flood):** 11,375–6,690 BCE.

## Not the synthesis date: 25,000 / 22,000

Kept per founder ("makes sense after all — leave it"):
- **25,000 yr** = Yahweh's age / Elohim technological lead / first arrival
  at the primordial ocean-Earth. NOT the human-synthesis date.
- **22,000 yr / ~21,810 BCE** = the Genesis-project inauguration (Capricorn
  opening). The duration-label looseness ("22,000 years ≈ one full Great
  Year") was reviewed and left as-is.
